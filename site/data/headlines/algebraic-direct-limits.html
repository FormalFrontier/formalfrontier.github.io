• Finite matrix units. Over a nonempty directed preorder of semirings with bundled ring-hom transitions, DirectLimit.matrixUnitsColimitEquiv identifies the limit of square-matrix unit groups with the matrix units over the coefficient limit. The dimension has Fintype and DecidableEq and may be empty; transitions need not be injective. See the matrix-units guide and source module.
  • Simultaneous finite-family representatives. Over a nonempty directed preorder, every finite family in a direct limit of types has representatives at one common stage. Transition maps may be arbitrary function-like maps; neither injectivity nor algebraic structure is needed. DirectLimit.forall_mk_eq_iff_exists characterizes equality of two finite families of quotient classes by equality of their transported functions at one common stage. Its _of_fixed variant bounds both supplied input stages, including for an empty family, without a separate nonempty-index assumption. See DirectedSystem and the first-order and paired-matrix examples.
  • Finite-support coefficient descent. Over a nonempty directed preorder, finitely supported functions commute with direct limits of types with zero whose selected transition maps preserve zero. The coefficient index may be arbitrary, and each function has a representative at one stage with exactly the same support. Neither injective transitions nor additive structure are required. See Finsupp.
  • Semilinear quotient descent. A continuous semilinear map from a module over a ring to a module over a semiring descends through any submodule in its kernel, retaining the original module structures and quotient topology. The codomain need only be an additive commutative monoid; continuity of module operations is not assumed. See Quotient.
  • Varying scalars and tensor products. For compatible semilinear module systems over a nonempty directed preorder, the tensor product of the two module limits over the varying commutative-semiring limit is equivalent to the limit of the stagewise tensor products. Transition maps need not be injective. See VaryingScalar.
  • Mittag--Leffler inverse systems. A pointwise nonempty, Type-valued Mittag--Leffler inverse system over a nonempty directed preorder with a countable cofinal subset has a compatible section. Under the same index and Mittag--Leffler conditions, each element of an eventual range lifts to a section without a separate pointwise-nonemptiness assumption. See MittagLeffler.
  • Prime spectra of filtered colimits. For a small filtered category and any colimit cocone of commutative rings, its spectrum cone is a limit in topological spaces. The chosen-limit homeomorphism identifies coordinates with contraction along the cocone maps; no injectivity or nonzero-ring assumption is required. See PrimeSpectrumFilteredColimits.
  • Krull dimension under finite-subset capture. For homomorphisms φ i : A i →+* B of commutative semirings, if every finite subset of B lies in the range of one φ i, then ringKrullDim B ≤ ⨆ i, ringKrullDim (A i). The family need not be directed and the maps need not be injective; dimensions include ⊥ and ⊤. See FiniteCapture.
  • Module topology on finite-dimensional strong duals. Over any normed field, the continuous dual of a finite-dimensional topological vector space with module topology also has module topology, retaining its topology of uniform convergence on bounded sets. Neither completeness nor a nontrivial norm on the field is required. This is an explicit theorem, not a global instance. See StrongDual.
  • Flat-coefficient tensor monomorphisms. Over a commutative ring with a supplied topology, tensoring the topological module factor with a flat algebraic coefficient module preserves injectivity of continuous linear maps and categorical monomorphisms. No coefficient topology is required. See TensorFunctor.
  • Coefficient tensor–continuous Hom adjunction. For a commutative ring with a supplied topology and a topological module with jointly continuous scalar action, coefficient tensor is left adjoint to the algebraic continuous-Hom functor, in a common carrier universe independent of the scalar universe. It preserves existing colimits of arbitrary diagram size; neither coefficients nor the Hom module are given a topology. See CoefficientTensor.
  • Continuous tensor quotients. For a commutative ring R with a supplied topology, an algebraic coefficient module C, a topological module M with jointly continuous scalar action and any submodule P, there is a continuous linear equivalence C ⊗[R] (M ⧸ P) ≃L[R] (C ⊗[R] M) ⧸ K, where K = LinearMap.range (TensorProduct.map LinearMap.id P.subtype). This is the range, not its closure; no flatness or closedness assumption is required. See Quotient.
  • Projective coefficients preserve tensor Hausdorffness. Over a commutative ring with a supplied topology, the one-sided tensor of a projective algebraic coefficient module with a Hausdorff topological module with jointly continuous scalar action is Hausdorff. No coefficient topology or finiteness assumption is required. See TensorSeparation.
  • Flat coefficients need not preserve tensor Hausdorffness. The one-sided tensor ℚ ⊗[ℤ] AddCircle (1 : ℝ) is not Hausdorff, although the rational coefficient module is flat and the usual real circle is compact Hausdorff with jointly continuous integer action. See the rational-circle counterexample.
