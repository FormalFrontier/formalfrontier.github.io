• Sigma-locally-finite basis criterion (TopologicalSpace.metrizableSpace_of_sigmaLocallyFinite_isTopologicalBasis). A T₃ space is metrizable if it has an open basis in countably many locally finite indexed levels. Each level may have an arbitrary index type; local finiteness counts the original indices, including repetitions.
  • Metric converse (TopologicalSpace.exists_sigmaLocallyFinite_isTopologicalBasis). Every metrizable space has an open basis in countably many locally finite levels, each indexed by the points of the space.
  • Closed metrizable neighbourhood-cover criterion (TopologicalSpace.metrizableSpace_of_locallyFinite_isClosed_interior_cover). A space is metrizable if it has a locally finite family of closed subsets whose interiors cover the space and each of which is metrizable in its original subtype topology. No ambient separation assumption is required.
