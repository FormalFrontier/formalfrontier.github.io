• Stable kernels and ranges. For an additive commutative group with its given module structure over a possibly zero, noncommutative semiring, simultaneous one-step stability at a natural bound, including zero, gives the specified sum equivalence from the power kernel and range. The kernel restriction is nilpotent and the range restriction is an automorphism. Canonical projections are independent of the stable bound and natural for arbitrary linear intertwiners. Noetherianity and Artinianity of the module give a common bound; the separate Noetherian-surjective and Artinian-injective automorphism criteria retain their asymmetric hypotheses. See StableKernelRange. Over arbitrary ring scalars, a finite-length module that is categorically indecomposable has a local endomorphism ring, and each endomorphism is nilpotent or a unit. The indecomposable predicate already excludes the zero module; see Local. The classical ring-module antecedents are Fitting's lemma and its indecomposable finite-length local-endomorphism consequence, as presented in Wisbauer, Foundations of Module and Ring Theory, §§31.13 and 32.4(3)(iii). The explicit-one-step semiring/additive-group decomposition above generalizes the cited ring-module setting; the local-endomorphism theorem retains ring scalars. See references and credit.
  • Internal Krull–Schmidt decompositions and cancellation. Over any ring, a Noetherian module or an Artinian module is a finite internal direct sum of nonzero indecomposable submodules; the two chain conditions are separate existence hypotheses. Each existence witness includes A i ≠ ⊥ alongside categorical indecomposability. Two supplied finite internal decompositions of linearly equivalent modules match up to an equivalence of index types and linear equivalences of summands when all summands have local endomorphism rings. Indecomposable summands and finite length of one total give the same matching. Both matching conclusions include ∀ i, A i ≠ ⊥ and ∀ j, B j ≠ ⊥, derived from the given hypotheses. Arbitrary finite index types use the caller's decidable equality instances and may be empty; existence uses the stock equality instance on Fin n. The matching is existential, not required to preserve the given coordinates. A common finite-length module P cancels from P × M ≃ₗ[R] P × N, with no chain condition on M or N; P may be zero. Zero modules, including those over a zero ring, have the empty decomposition. See KrullSchmidt, which realizes the categorical theorems of Homological Algebra through ModuleCat.

  • Finiteness, projectivity and flatness under pure scalar extension. Let R : Type uR be a commutative ring, S an R-algebra and M : Type uM an additive commutative monoid with its given R-module structure. Suppose the canonical map N → S ⊗[R] N, n ↦ 1 ⊗ n, is injective for every additive commutative R-semimodule N : Type (max uR uM). Then Module.Finite.iff_finite_tensorProduct_of_injective gives Module.Finite S (S ⊗[R] M) ↔ Module.Finite R M for any Semiring S, and Module.FinitePresentation.iff_finitePresentation_tensorProduct_of_injective gives the corresponding finite-presentation equivalence for any Ring S. These scalar algebras need not be commutative. The submodule comparisons reflect membership and inclusions and characterize finite generation of scalar-extension images, without assuming tensoring preserves submodule injections. If S is commutative, Module.Flat.iff_flat_tensorProduct_of_injective also gives Module.Flat S (S ⊗[R] M) ↔ Module.Flat R M. The tensor has its canonical left-factor S-action; M needs no S-action or supplied additive group structure. All three carrier universes are independent. No flatness of S over R is assumed. Module.finite_projective_of_tensorProduct_of_injective descends finite projectivity for any possibly noncommutative Semiring S: if S ⊗[R] M is finite and projective over S, then M is finite and projective over R. Finite presentation of M is derived, not assumed. The clients also retain the commutative-target proof using finitely presented flat modules and apply the noncommutative result using a linear scalar retraction.

  • Annihilators under finite-limit-preserving scalar extension. For a CommSemiring R, a possibly noncommutative Semiring S with Algebra R S, and a finitely generated semimodule M, Module.annihilator_tensorProduct_eq_map_of_finite_of_preservesFiniteLimits gives

    Module.annihilator S (S ⊗[R] M) =
  Ideal.map (algebraMap R S) (Module.annihilator R M)

    provided the stock SemimoduleCat tensor-left functor for the lifted S preserves finite limits over the lifted R, at the common universe max uR (max uS uM). The conclusion uses the original S and original tensor product; the three original universes are independent. Here finite means Module.Finite R M, not a finite carrier. No additive cancellation, subtractivity, faithful action, finite presentation or Noetherianity is required. This condition is not identified with mono-flatness over semirings. Over commutative rings, flatness supplies this condition through the existing module–semimodule equivalence. For any module M over a commutative ring R and a flat, possibly noncommutative R-algebra S, Module.annihilator_tensorProduct_eq_iInf_map_of_flat identifies the annihilator of S ⊗[R] M with the intersection of the ideals generated by the images of Module.annihilator R N, over all finitely generated original submodules N ≤ M. This is not a claim that ideal extension commutes with arbitrary intersections. For finite M, Module.annihilator_tensorProduct_eq_map_of_finite_of_flat identifies it with the ideal generated by Module.annihilator R M itself. The tensor has its canonical left-factor action; M needs no S-action. S need only be a semiring and M an additive commutative monoid with the given structures. The three universes are independent. No Noetherianity, finite presentation, flatness or faithfulness of M, or faithful flatness of S is required. The clients evaluate the intersection for the nonfinite free abelian group ℕ →₀ ℤ after rational scalar extension and recover finite faithful-action preservation over ring coefficients. The existing FaithfulSMul.baseChange_of_finite_of_flat additionally allows commutative semiring coefficients.

  • Annihilator contraction under faithfully flat extension. Over a CommRing R, Module.FaithfullyFlat.annihilator_tensorProduct_eq says tensoring an arbitrary R-module M with a faithfully flat module P preserves its annihilator over R. Here P is only a module group, while M needs only an additive commutative monoid structure. For a faithfully flat, possibly noncommutative ring algebra S, Module.comap_annihilator_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat identifies contraction of the annihilator of S ⊗[R] M with the original annihilator. The tensor has its canonical left-factor action and M needs no S-action. All three carrier universes are independent, with no finiteness, flatness or faithfulness assumption on M. The module client derives preservation and reflection of faithful action over the coefficient ring; this does not assert faithful action over a larger scalar algebra.
  • Faithfully flat tensor products. Over a CommRing R, for additive commutative groups C and D with R-module structures, Module.FaithfullyFlat.tensorProduct_iff says that C ⊗[R] D is faithfully flat over R if and only if both C and D are faithfully flat over R. The ring and the two module universes are independent; no finiteness, projectivity or nontriviality assumption is needed.
  • Faithfully flat dependent direct sums. For arbitrary families of additive commutative groups with module structures over a CommRing R, Module.FaithfullyFlat.directSum_iff characterizes faithful flatness of the direct sum by flatness of every component and a nontrivial quotient-tensor fiber in some component for each proper ideal of R. The detecting component may depend on the ideal; the components need not individually be faithfully flat. The ring, index and carrier universes are independent, and empty indexing types are allowed. One faithfully flat component is sufficient when every component is flat.
  • Finitely generated ideals and dependent products. Over a CommSemiring R, Submodule.smul_pi_of_fg says that multiplication by a finitely generated ideal commutes with the full dependent product of submodules of additive commutative R-modules. The index type is arbitrary, including empty, and the semiring, index and carrier universes are independent.
  • Faithful flatness after base change covering maximal ideals. For commutative rings R and S with an R-algebra structure on S, suppose every maximal ideal m of R satisfies m.map (algebraMap R S) ≠ ⊤. For any additive commutative group M with an R-module structure, Module.FaithfullyFlat.iff_flat_and_baseChange_of_map_maximal_ne_top characterizes faithful flatness over R by original flatness Module.Flat R M and faithful flatness over S of S ⊗[R] M, with the canonical left tensor-factor action. The three universes are independent. No flatness of S over R, surjectivity, algebra structure on M, finiteness, Noetherianity, localness, topology or completion is assumed. For I ≤ Ideal.jacobson (⊥ : Ideal R), Module.FaithfullyFlat.iff_flat_and_baseChange_of_le_jacobson specializes this to the canonical quotient R ⧸ I. For commutative R-algebras S, Module.FaithfullyFlat.iff_flat_and_quotient_of_le_jacobson gives the equivalent formulation using the canonical quotient algebra R ⧸ I → S ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R S). Conversely, Algebra.map_maximal_ne_top_iff_forall_faithfullyFlat_baseChange characterizes the maximal-ideal hypothesis itself: it holds if and only if scalar extension preserves and reflects faithful flatness for every originally flat R-module in Type (max u w), where R : Type u and w is arbitrary. The universe of S remains independent. The converse uses the direct sum of principal localizations indexed by all elements of a maximal ideal, without assuming that ideal is finitely generated. The quotient client applies this corollary to the nonsurjective rank-two diagonal over ZMod 4 with the proper nonzero square-zero ideal (2). Its inputs are obtained independently from basis-derived faithful flatness.
  • Faithful flatness of unital algebras. For a CommRing R and a unital R-algebra A whose underlying ring may be noncommutative, Module.FaithfullyFlat.algebra_iff_flat_and_fg_proper_ideal characterizes faithful flatness by flatness and I • (⊤ : Submodule R A) ≠ ⊤ for every finitely generated proper ideal I. The algebra structure is material; this finitely generated ideal criterion is not asserted for arbitrary modules.
  • Faithfully flat products of algebras. Over a CommRing R, for an arbitrary dependent family of unital, possibly noncommutative R-algebras A i, Module.FaithfullyFlat.pi_algebra_iff characterizes faithful flatness of Π i, A i by flatness of the whole product and a nontrivial fiber (R ⧸ I) ⊗[R] A i in some component for every finitely generated proper ideal I. The detecting component may depend on the ideal. Componentwise flatness cannot replace whole-product flatness, and the components need not individually be faithfully flat. The ring, index and carrier universes are independent, and empty products are allowed.
  • Products of localizations at submonoids. For a CommRing R, arbitrary submonoids S i and commutative R-algebras A i realizing the localizations at S i, Module.FaithfullyFlat.pi_localization_iff characterizes faithful flatness of Π i, A i by flatness of the whole product and, for every finitely generated proper ideal I, disjointness of I from some S i. These are products with the common diagonal R-action; neither finite nor nonempty indexing is required, and zero rings are allowed.
  • Families of principal localizations. For a CommRing R and any family s : ι → R, Module.FaithfullyFlat.directSum_away_iff characterizes faithful flatness of ⨁ i, Localization.Away (s i) by Ideal.span (Set.range s) = ⊤, without finite or nonempty indexing. For finite index types, Module.FaithfullyFlat.pi_away_iff gives the same unit-span criterion for Π i, Localization.Away (s i). The localizations need not individually be faithfully flat; zero rings and independent ring and index universes are allowed. The finite-index hypothesis for the product unit-span criterion is essential: the coordinate-idempotent product is faithfully flat although its generators span a proper ideal. The ideal-detection consequences show that this ideal is not finitely generated.
  • Universal coherence. IsUniversallyCoherent asserts coherence for finitely presented commutative algebras in an independently chosen target universe; the library derives base coherence at v = u and supplies the Noetherian case.
  • Square-zero kernels and projection base change. For a commutative semiring R and an additive commutative R-module M, TrivSqZeroExt.kerIdealLinearEquiv identifies the projection kernel with M. If M is finite over R, kerIdeal_finite makes the kernel finite over the whole extension. kerIdealBaseChange identifies the projection-base-changed kernel with M without a finiteness assumption; its pure-tensor and inverse formulas are simp lemmas.
  • Left Noetherian square-zero extensions. For a possibly noncommutative Ring R and an AddCommMonoid M with commuting left and right R-module actions, TrivSqZeroExt.isNoetherianRing_iff characterizes IsNoetherianRing (TrivSqZeroExt R M) by IsNoetherianRing R ∧ Module.Finite R M. Finiteness here is as a left R-module. The companion TrivSqZeroExt.isNoetherianRing_iff_isNoetherian replaces Module.Finite R M by IsNoetherian R M. Centrality is not required, and the two carrier universes are independent.
  • Finite-type ideal/module transfer. Over a commutative base ring, Ideal.ideal_property_iff_module_property equates a property on all finitely generated ideals with one on all finite modules over finite-type algebras. The property must be invariant under compatible algebra/module isomorphisms and descend along every compatible surjective square-zero algebra map for finite source modules. The base universe is independent of the common algebra/module universe. Finite type does not mean module-finite over the base, and arbitrary ideals need not be finitely generated.

The foundation, finite-presentation and coherent-module category results allow arbitrary Ring. Coherent semilinear Hom allows arbitrary source and target scalar Semirings with a commuting Ring action on the target. Localization of coherent modules, coherent tensor closure, and universal coherence use CommRing. The square-zero kernel and base-change results use CommSemiring and AddCommMonoid; the square-zero Noetherian criterion allows Ring and AddCommMonoid with commuting bimodule actions. The predicate transfer uses CommRing and AddCommGroup. The ideal/product identity and semilinear mono-flatness invariance use CommSemiring and AddCommMonoid; the faithful-flatness criteria use CommRing and require no coherence or projectivity assumption. Module carrier universes are independent where stated. Zero rings/modules, empty indexing sets and rank-zero finite free modules are not excluded. Consult the linked source statements and guide for exact hypotheses. This is not a coherent-sheaf theory or a claim to formalize an entire source.
