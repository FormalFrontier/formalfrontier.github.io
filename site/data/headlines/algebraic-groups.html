• Cartier's theorem. Every commutative Hopf algebra over a characteristic-zero field is reduced, and is smooth if it is finite type, for its given coefficient algebra structure and with independent scalar and carrier universes. See Hopf algebra smoothness.
  • Linear embeddings of affine algebraic groups. Every affine group scheme locally of finite type over a field K admits a group-scheme morphism G → GLₙ for some n : ℕ whose underlying scheme morphism is a closed immersion. No basis or representation is supplied, and nonreduced group schemes are included. See the closed linear embedding theorem.
  • Surjectivity and faithfully flat coordinates. For homomorphisms of given affine group schemes locally of finite type, dominance over an algebraically closed field implies surjectivity on all scheme points; over any field, schematic dominance suffices. Over any field with reduced target H, dominance or injectivity of the compatible coordinate map A → B gives Module.FaithfullyFlat A B for its given algebra structure. The coordinate presentations may have independent universes. For finite-type commutative Hopf algebras over any field, an injective morphism f : A →ₐc[K] B with reduced source coordinate algebra A satisfies f.toAlgHom.toRingHom.FaithfullyFlat, by BialgHom.faithfullyFlat_of_injective_of_isReduced. This uses the given Hopf structures, in independent scalar and carrier universes. See surjectivity over algebraically closed fields, schematic dominance over fields, and faithful flatness of coordinates.
  • Hopf tensor–quotient equivalences. For a central bialgebra map from a commutative bialgebra A over a commutative ring R to a possibly noncommutative Hopf algebra B, comultiplication gives a first-factor B-linear equivalence B ⊗[A] B ≃ₗ[B] B ⊗[R] (B / (ker ε_A)B). In this generality the quotient is only a module quotient, and A need not have an antipode. When B is commutative, HopfAlgebra.tensorQuotientAlgEquiv refines the same map to a B-algebra equivalence on the canonical tensor and quotient algebras. The simp equality tensorQuotientAlgEquiv_toLinearEquiv recovers tensorQuotientEquiv, so its finite-comultiplication formulas compute both directions. For a group-like c, the inverse sends (b c) ⊗ [c] to b ⊗ c. Mixed scalar extension is the composition of inverse tensor cancellation, congruence with this refinement, and tensor cancellation. Compatible ordinary algebra maps and scalar towers suffice; the commutative coefficient algebra needs no Hopf structure. See HopfAlgebra/TensorQuotient.
  • Finite Hopf algebras and Hopf modules. Every finite-dimensional Hopf algebra over a field has a linear functional λ such that λ (a * x) is nondegenerate in both arguments. Its left regular module is injective (HopfAlgebra.injective_self). The algebra need not be commutative or cocommutative, and the field may have any characteristic. See the Frobenius and self-injectivity theorems. For decomposition over a commutative semiring R, let H be a possibly noncommutative semiring with full Hopf algebra structure, and X a specified right comodule on an additive-commutative-monoid R-module M. For its given right action with IsScalarTower R Hᵐᵒᵖ M, an explicit hX : X.IsHopfModule gives X.coinvariantsTensorEquiv hX : (X.weightSpace (1 : H)) ⊗[R] H ≃ₗ[R] M. The forward map is the given action. The inverse X.toCoinvariantsTensor hX corestricts the antipode contraction to coinvariants before tensoring it with H and composing with the coaction. Both inverse identities hold, including the tensor-side identity inside coinvariants tensored with H itself. Both maps intertwine the given right action with right multiplication on the H factor. Universes are independent; no flatness, finiteness or inverse antipode is required. See the decomposition and its API.
