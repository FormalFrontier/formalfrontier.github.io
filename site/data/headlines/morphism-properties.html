The pullback declarations below lie in CategoryTheory.MorphismProperty. In the one-factor results, f : S' ⟶ S is a fixed base-change arrow. For structure maps v₁₂ : X ⟶ S, v₂₂ : Y ⟶ S, and g : X ⟶ Y with v₁₂ = g ≫ v₂₂, the induced map is X ×[S] S' ⟶ Y ×[S] S'. In the two-factor result, instead, f : X ⟶ S and g : Y ⟶ S are structure arrows into the common base, as are f' : X' ⟶ S and g' : Y' ⟶ S. Maps i₁ : X ⟶ X' and i₂ : Y ⟶ Y' satisfying f = i₁ ≫ f' and g = i₂ ≫ g' induce Mathlib's pullback.map : X ×[S] Y ⟶ X' ×[S] Y'.

  • Preservation along a target projection (pullbackLift_fst_snd_of_isStableUnderBaseChangeAlong). If P g and P is stable under base change along Y ×[S] S' ⟶ Y, the induced map has property P. Only the two endpoint pullbacks are required.
  • Relative preservation along an E arrow (pullbackLift_fst_snd_of_isStableUnderBaseChangeAgainst). Assume P.IsStableUnderBaseChangeAgainst E, E.IsStableUnderBaseChange, E f, and P g, with the two endpoint pullbacks. Then the induced map has P.
  • Relative slice-functor preservation (overPullbackMap_of_isStableUnderBaseChangeAgainst). Under the same stability and base-arrow hypotheses and Limits.HasPullbacksAlong f, Mathlib's existing CategoryTheory.Over.pullback f preserves P on underlying horizontal arrows.
  • Object-structured preservation (pullbackLift_fst_snd_of_intersection_of_postcomp). Assume (P ⊓ E).IsStableUnderBaseChange, E.HasOfPostcompProperty E, E v₁₂, E v₂₂, and P g. With the two endpoint pullbacks, the induced map belongs to P ⊓ E along arbitrary f, without E f or absolute stability of P or E.
  • One-factor preservation alternatives (pullbackLift_fst_snd_of_relative_alternatives). Assume stability of P against E, absolute stability of P ⊓ E and of E, cancellation of E under postcomposition by E, and P g. With the endpoint pullbacks, the induced map has P if either both structure maps belong to E or f belongs to E.
  • Two-factor relative preservation (pullbackMap_of_relative_alternatives). Assume composition closure of P, stability of P against E, absolute stability of P ⊓ E and E, and cancellation of E under postcomposition by E. If P i₁ and P i₂, the canonical map has P provided either E f ∧ E f' or E f ∧ E g'. Besides the endpoint pullbacks of (f, g) and (f', g'), only the mixed pullback selected by the alternative is required: (f', g) in the first case, (f, g') in the second. No identity closure of P is assumed.
  • Absolute two-factor specialization (pullbackMap_of_relative_alternatives_top). With E = ⊤, absolute base-change stability and composition closure of P, P i₁, P i₂, and the same commuting equations suffice under Mathlib's pullbackMap hypotheses Limits.HasPullbacksAlong f and Limits.HasPullbacksAlong g'. The conclusion is membership for the same canonical map. No pullbacks along g or identity closure are added.
  • Either-leg criterion for existing squares (isStableUnderBaseChange_either_iff). For base-change-stable E, preservation of P on an existing square IsPullback p q a b whenever P a and E a ∨ E b is equivalent to P.IsStableUnderBaseChangeAgainst E and (P ⊓ E).IsStableUnderBaseChange. The preserved arrow is q; no pullback-existence assumption is made.
  • Canonical structured-over transport (Over.pullbackOfIntersectionOfPostcomp). A functor E.Over P S ⥤ E.Over P S' assuming P.IsMultiplicative, E.HasPullbacksAlong f, E.IsStableUnderBaseChangeAlong f, (P ⊓ E).IsStableUnderBaseChange, and E.HasOfPostcompProperty E. Here existence is required only for structure arrows satisfying E, not for every object over S.
  • Fixed-base pullback pairing (Over.pullbackPairing and Over.pullbackPairing_map_mem_of_relative). A functor (E.Over P S × Q.Over P S) ⥤ CategoryTheory.Over S assuming only P.IsMultiplicative and HasPullback A.hom B.hom for every selected A : E.Over P S, B : Q.Over P S. No closure of E or Q is required. Objects are the chosen ambient pullbacks and maps are the canonical pullback.map. Preservation of P separately assumes stability of P against E, base-change stability of P ⊓ E and E, and cancellation of E under postcomposition by E. The left endpoints supply E; the selected family supplies the mixed pullback (target-left, source-right).
  • Product of the forgetful input functors (Over.pullbackPairingFan_isLimit). Under the same multiplicativity and selected-pair existence hypotheses, the pullback pairing with its two projection transformations is a binary product of Prod.fst ⋙ Over.forget E P S and Prod.snd ⋙ Over.forget Q P S in (E.Over P S × Q.Over P S) ⥤ CategoryTheory.Over S. Joint lifts and projections are ordinary-over morphisms; no membership in P is imposed.
  • Symmetry of fixed-base pairing (Over.pullbackPairingSymmetry). With the same multiplicativity and selected-pair existence hypotheses, interchanging E and Q gives a natural isomorphism to the reversed pairing. Reverse pullbacks are derived from the forward family. Components are the ambient pullback symmetry maps, with characteristic equations for both projections, the inverse, and swapping twice. No stability or iso invariance of the morphism properties is required.
  • Density under slicing (CategoryTheory.CostructuredArrow.isDenseAt_toOver and isDense_toOver). For arbitrary categories A, C, a functor F : A ⥤ C, X : C, and Y : CategoryTheory.Over X, F.isDenseAt Y.left implies (CategoryTheory.CostructuredArrow.toOver F X).isDenseAt Y. Consequently, if F.IsDense, then CostructuredArrow.toOver F X is dense. No fullness, faithfulness, limit-existence or nonemptiness hypothesis is required; a converse to the pointwise implication is not asserted.

The transport API characterizes objects, structure maps, underlying maps and both projections, supplies membership in P and P ⊓ E, and gives the ambient pullback universal property and uniqueness of the structured map and functor lift. Identity and composition equations come from the assembled functor laws. Under absolute stability of P, the functor agrees with Mathlib's structured pullback. Under Limits.HasPullbacksAlong f, its forgetful factorization has Mathlib's ordinary over-category pullback comparison isomorphism.
