• Natural norms. For a commutative scalar ring and finite group, the norm from coinvariants to invariants is natural in the coefficient representation. For a finite normal subgroup of an arbitrary ambient group, the residual-quotient-equivariant norm is natural as well; the ambient group need not be finite or commutative. This module permits independent scalar, group and coefficient universes.
  • Exceptional degrees. The exact norm sequence Tate⁻¹ → M_G → M^G → Tate⁰ identifies degrees −1 and 0 naturally with the norm's kernel and cokernel. Both natural isomorphisms (including their component identities) are shipped, not proposed; this layer inherits the same-universe boundary of the pinned Tate-complex API.
  • Fixed-element coefficient functors. For an element g of a finite commutative group, the even and odd alternating N/g−1 homology models are functorial in coefficients. This construction does not require g to generate the group.
  • Objectwise additive period two. With a chosen element satisfying ∀ x, x ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g, the all-integer Tate comparisons give period-two isomorphisms. Same-parity comparisons have identity, inverse and telescoping laws; these Tate comparisons are objectwise, not claimed natural in the coefficient representation.
  • Hilbert's theorem 90. For a finite Galois extension L/K and a specified automorphism generating the full Galois group, every norm-one element of L is y / g(y) for a unit y of the original field. The field endpoint permits independent universes for K and L, with no characteristic or invertible-degree assumption. It reuses Mathlib's cocycle theorem and finite-cyclic calculation.
