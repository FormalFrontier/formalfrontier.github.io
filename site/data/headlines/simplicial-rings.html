• PolyRing R n has n central indeterminates over any Ring R; homogeneous coordinate values sum to one. substitute evaluates free generators at whole-target-center elements (a sufficient, not necessarily minimal, condition); hom_ext determines ring maps by coefficients and generators.
  • simplexFunctor sends a ring to an actual simplicial ring, with contravariant ordinalMap for every ordinal arrow, including empty fibres. Polynomial constantInclusion followed by evalAtZero is strictly the identity on the unextended simplex; both maps are coefficient-natural.
  • contraction is a native seven-identity SimplicialObject.Homotopy from evaluation-then-inclusion to the identity on the polynomially extended simplex. Its first face is the identity endpoint and its last face is evaluation-then-inclusion; homotopyMap_natural handles every unital coefficient map. Whiskering along a supplied covariant functor uses mathlib's existing Homotopy.whiskerRight, not a new wrapper here.

See the simplex guide and homotopy guide for the precise coordinate, degree, face, degeneracy and universe conventions.
