  • Subset-weight-one valuations have full rational residue fields. For any field, any variable type and subset containing a chosen pivot, the weight-one construction identifies the multivariate function field birationally with a univariate rational function field over the field of unweighted variables and pivot ratios. Minimum subset weight gives a surjective rank-one discrete valuation; its pivot is a uniformizer, and its entire residue field is the rational function field in those unweighted variables and ratios. The equal-order fraction law computes residues from initial coefficients. For the empty subset, the separate trivial valuation has no discrete-valuation claim.
  • Hilbert basis theorem for left-Noetherian rings. Polynomial.isNoetherianRing_of_isNoetherianRing proves IsNoetherianRing (Polynomial R) for any R : Type u with [Ring R] and [IsNoetherianRing R]. The polynomial variable is central; coefficient commutativity, nontriviality and absence of zero divisors are not required. On commutative rings, Polynomial.isNoetherianRing_of_isNoetherianRing_eq_isNoetherianRing identifies this proof with Mathlib's Polynomial.isNoetherianRing. For any finite type σ : Type v, the named theorem AddMonoidAlgebra.isNoetherianRing_finsupp proves left Noetherianity of AddMonoidAlgebra R (σ →₀ ℕ), with independently polymorphic universes. It permits zero and noncommutative coefficient rings. For commutative rings, this is definitionally MvPolynomial σ R. Here IsNoetherianRing R means IsNoetherian R R for the canonical left self-action. It does not assume IsNoetherian R (Polynomial R): that coefficient-module property is false for every nontrivial coefficient ring. The examples include the noncommutative 16-element ring Matrix (Fin 2) (Fin 2) (ZMod 2), whose polynomial ring is infinite but left-Noetherian, the zero ring ZMod 1, and ZMod 4 with its nonzero nilpotent 2.
  • Noetherian polynomial-ring criteria. The Noetherian module proves the coefficient-and-variable characterization over arbitrary commutative rings: MvPolynomial σ R is Noetherian for its self-action exactly when R is Noetherian and either σ is finite or R is the zero ring. For nontrivial commutative semirings, Noetherianity of the polynomial semiring forces σ to be finite; no semiring Hilbert-basis sufficiency is asserted. The nontrivial-ring form removes the zero-ring alternative without imposing a domain hypothesis. The coefficient and variable universes are independent. The examples distinguish empty, finite and infinite variables over the integers, ZMod 6, and ZMod 1.
  • Artinian polynomial-semiring criteria. For any R : Type u with [Semiring R], including noncommutative coefficients, Polynomial.isArtinianRing_iff proves IsArtinianRing (Polynomial R) ↔ Subsingleton R. For arbitrary variable types σ : Type u and coefficients R : Type v with [CommSemiring R], MvPolynomial.isArtinianRing_iff proves IsArtinianRing (MvPolynomial σ R) ↔ IsArtinianRing R ∧ (IsEmpty σ ∨ Subsingleton R). Both use polynomial multiplication on the canonical left self-module, not the coefficient R-module action. All universes are arbitrary; the multivariate universes are independent. No ring or finite-variable hypothesis is imposed. Empty multivariate variables recover coefficient Artinianity; trivial coefficients make every polynomial zero. The univariate applications include the usual non-ring semiring ℝ≥0, the noncommutative 16-element matrix ring over ZMod 2, and the zero ring ZMod 1.
  • Univariate coefficient-module Noetherianity and Artinianity each characterize trivial coefficients. For any R : Type u with [Semiring R], Polynomial.isNoetherian_iff and Polynomial.isArtinian_iff give IsNoetherian R (Polynomial R) ↔ Subsingleton R and IsArtinian R (Polynomial R) ↔ Subsingleton R, respectively. Both use the canonical left coefficient action r • p = Polynomial.C r * p, not polynomial multiplication as a self-module. The universe is arbitrary; no commutativity, ring, domain or field hypothesis is required. The coefficient-module examples distinguish these properties from coefficient self-Noetherianity and self-Artinianity, even for the infinite non-ring semiring ℝ≥0 with finitely many ideals.
  • Radical coefficient-ideal extensions are characterized by subtractivity. For any commutative semiring R, ideal I, and nonempty variable type σ, MvPolynomial.isRadical_map_C_iff says that I.map C is radical exactly when I is radical and satisfies a ∈ I → a + b ∈ I → b ∈ I. The sufficiency theorem also allows empty variable types. The coefficient and variable universes are independent; no properness, ring, domain, reducedness or finiteness hypothesis is needed. The examples include the proper nonzero kernel of reduction ℕ →+* ZMod 2 and the original prime-extension and radical-extension necessity applications.
  • Coefficient-and-variable ideals have an exact finite-generation criterion. For any commutative semiring R, coefficient ideal I, and variable subset S, MvPolynomial.ideal_map_C_sup_span_X_image_fg_iff characterizes finite generation of Ideal.map C I ⊔ Ideal.span (X '' S): I must be finitely generated, and either S is finite or I is the unit ideal. The coefficient and variable universes are independent; no ring, domain, subtractivity or finite-ambient-variable hypothesis is required. The examples include a proper nonsubtractive ideal over ℕ, coefficient zero divisors, and the zero-ring boundary.
  • The variable ideal is not finitely generated. For a nontrivial commutative semiring and infinitely many variables, MvPolynomial.idealOfVars_not_fg proves non-finite generation. A finite union of polynomial supports misses some variable; evaluation separating that variable gives a contradiction. Mathlib supplies the complementary finite-variable ideal theorem. This library does not prove the motivating complete short exact sequence.
  • Antichain coefficients of monomial ideals. For any commutative semiring R, arbitrary variable type σ and antichain S : Set (σ →₀ ℕ), MvPolynomial.monomialIdealCoeffMap restricts coefficients from the stock ideal generated by the monic monomials with exponents in S to S →₀ R. It is surjective and semilinear over constantCoeff, for the full polynomial-semiring action on the ideal and the coefficient action on the target. Its coordinate and monomial laws give the original coefficients and the corresponding single functions. Coefficients and variables have independent universes; empty or infinite antichains and variable types, and trivial coefficients, are allowed. The coordinate-axes example records the three exponents of (xy, xz, yz) over ℂ, their antichain and monomial presentation, and the ideal's nonzeroness and properness. For finite S and nontrivial R, MvPolynomial.spanFinrank_ideal_span_monomial proves that the ideal's minimum number of polynomial generators is S.ncard. MvPolynomial.card_le_card_of_span_eq_ideal_span_monomial bounds every finite generating set, not only sets of monomials. This is generation over the full polynomial semiring, not coefficient-module dimension. The coordinate-axes example consequently has minimum number of generators three.
  • Variable classes form the augmentation cotangent basis. For any (σ : Type u) and (R : Type v) [CommRing R], MvPolynomial.idealOfVarsCotangentBasis is a σ-indexed R-basis of the stock variable-ideal cotangent I/I². Its vectors are the classes of X i, with inherited coefficient action r • [p] = [C r * p]. The _apply and _repr_X lemmas characterize those vectors, and idealOfVarsCotangentBasis_repr_toCotangent computes each coordinate as (x : MvPolynomial σ R).coeff (Finsupp.single i 1) for any representative x in the variable ideal. Quadratic and higher terms do not contribute. The variable and coefficient universes are independent; empty or infinite variable types, zero rings and nilpotent coefficients are allowed. This is an indexed basis, not a numerical rank assertion over the zero ring.
  • Degree-controlled finite interpolation. Over any commutative ring R, let s : Finset ι select points a : ι → σ → R. If, for every distinct i, j ∈ s, the coordinate differences a i x - a j x span the unit ideal, then any prescribed values v : ι → R are attained on s by a polynomial of total degree at most s.card - 1 (MvPolynomial.exists_eval_eq_totalDegree_le_of_pairwise_span_eq_top). Both the variable type and ambient label type may be infinite; no field or domain hypothesis is needed. The theorem asserts existence, not uniqueness.
  • Comparison with Lagrange interpolation. Over a field, for a unique variable type, a polynomial taking prescribed values at distinct nodes and having total degree strictly below the number of nodes maps to Mathlib's Lagrange interpolant under the canonical single-variable equivalence (MvPolynomial.uniqueAlgEquiv_eq_interpolate_of_eval_eq). The univariate degree bound follows from the total-degree bound. On a nonempty selection, the bound s.card - 1 also suffices, so the comparison applies to any bounded witness supplied by the commutative-ring existence theorem. Zero agrees with the empty interpolant separately; an arbitrary degree-zero witness need not do so.
