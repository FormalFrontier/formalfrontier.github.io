• Integral all-simplex chain coordinates (chainGroupIsoFinsupp, chainComplexIsoFinsupp). For any ring R, AlgebraicTopology.finsuppChainComplexFunctor R gives the ordinary-Finsupp alternating-face chain complex, with independent ring and simplex universes. Over ℤ, chainComplexIsoFinsupp is a natural isomorphism from the existing SSet.chainComplexFunctor at the original coefficient object (ModuleCat.uliftFunctor.{u} ℤ).obj (ModuleCat.of ℤ ℤ) to this functor. For every X : SSet.{u} and degree n, its evaluation isomorphism is exactly chainGroupIsoFinsupp X n: both forward and inverse degree maps are the original maps on every chain, not merely on generators. The two summand laws still compute lifted integers and unit basis chains. Naturality holds for every simplicial map and every chain; pushforwards add coefficients of coincident images, without finite-fiber or injectivity assumptions. In every degree the boundary coordinates are the signed sum of face pushforwards. This comparison concerns all simplices, not normalized chains or homology. The standard-point chain is nonzero; the identity edge of Δ[1] has boundary [v₁] - [v₀], nonzero as a chain, not as a homology class.
  • Augmented left décalage and its canonical extra degeneracy (LeftDecalage). For every simplicial object in any category, P(X)ₙ = Xₙ₊₁ augments to X₀, with ε₀ = X.δ 1, not in general the ordinary projection X.δ 0. No terminal/zero objects, additivity or limits are required. Functoriality and naturality are explicit; Mathlib supplies the section and combinatorial homotopy.
  • Finite-fiber transfers and coefficient comparison (FiniteFiberTransfer). In a preadditive category with simplex-indexed coproducts, equifibered simplicial maps with finite vertex fibers admit reverse-composing transfers. No fixed sheet number is needed. A constant vertex-fiber cardinality d gives pushforward after transfer equal to d • id on chains and, with homology, in every degree. Additive functors preserving those coproducts commute with transfer; no exactness or homology preservation is assumed. Pullback and coefficient laws are included.
  • Normalized descent and change of coefficient category (Normalization). Morphisms between split simplicial objects in a preadditive category descend without preserving chosen nondegenerate summands. For preadditive coefficient categories with HasCoproducts.{w}, additive functors preserving exactly Discrete T colimits for T : Type w compare normalized chains naturally, compatibly with projection, section, transfers and natural transformations. All five category/simplex universes are independent; no exactness or Abelian assumption is needed. These are distinct from the integral tensor presentation.
  • Integral tensor chains, simplicial maps and homotopies (TensorCoefficients, TensorMaps, TensorHomotopies). For every abelian group A and simplicial set S, the independently defined alternating tensor boundary gives a complex naturally isomorphic to normalized chains with lifted coefficients. Coefficient, simplex and padding universes are independent; no finiteness, flatness, field or nonemptiness is assumed. Simplicial monomorphisms give monic chain maps without flatness of A. Combinatorial or cylinder homotopies give chain homotopies and equal homology maps; two-sided simplicial homotopy inverses and extra-degenerate augmentations give quasi-isomorphisms. For normalized chains the coefficient category need only be preadditive with coproducts and homology; augmentation targets need not be terminal. Cokernel comparison applies to all arrows; its nonzero omitted-vertex example concerns chain cokernels, not homology.
  • Additive and exact change of abelian coefficients (TensorCoefficientMaps, TensorCoefficientFunctor, TensorCoefficientSequences). Arbitrary additive maps act by id ⊗ g, naturally in both variables, on the original tensor carriers and boundary. The coefficient-curried functor is additive and preserves finite limits and colimits. Any exact coefficient pair stays exact degreewise; injective and surjective endpoints give chain Mono/Epi and short exactness. Universes remain independent with common balanced padding; no coefficient flatness or endpoint assumption is added to middle exactness. This is exactness of a chain-valued functor, not exactness after homology.
  • Degree-zero homology, components and path components (TensorHomologyZero, SingularHomologyZero). For every abelian A and simplicial set S, tensor H₀ is the coproduct of ModuleCat.of ℤ (ULift.{max w p} A) over SSet.π₀ S, naturally in simplicial and additive coefficient maps, with independent a, w, p and no finiteness, flatness, field, basis or nonemptiness hypotheses. For singular simplices of any topological space this gives the coproduct over ZerothHomotopy X, naturally in continuous and coefficient maps, without local path connectedness. The direct path-component functor agrees naturally with singular components via Mathlib's equivalence.
  • Degree-zero normalized homology of binary products (tensorNormalizedHomologyZeroProductIso). For arbitrary abelian groups A, B and simplicial sets K, L, H₀ᴺ(K; A) ⊗ℤ H₀ᴺ(L; B) ≅ H₀ᴺ(K × L; A ⊗ℤ B), using canonical universe lifts with independent coefficient, simplex and padding universes. Pure tensors of vertex classes map to the paired vertex class with pure tensor coefficient; inverse formulas, extensionality and uniqueness characterize the maps. Forward and inverse naturality hold for simplicial maps in both factors and arbitrary additive maps in both coefficient groups, with balanced padding for coefficient change. No finiteness, flatness, connectedness or nonemptiness is assumed. A discrete two-by-two example distinguishes the four nonzero vertex tensors and their four product classes, and evaluates simplicial and coefficient maps on them.
  • Component folds, terminal sets and dimension vanishing (TensorHomologyZeroConnected, TensorHomologyZeroEmpty, PointHomology). The fold agrees with collapse and terminal coefficient evaluation for every source, including the empty one. Connectedness makes the fold an isomorphism. Terminal sets have the lifted coefficient object in degree zero and zero positive homology. More generally S.HasDimensionLT d gives vanishing in degrees n ≥ d; H₀(∂Δ[0]) is zero. Arbitrary abelian coefficients and independent coefficient/simplex/padding universes are retained.
  • Coefficient naturality of ordinary simplicial H₀ (HomologyZero). Mathlib's SSet.homology₀Iso and its inverse commute with every coefficient morphism in any preadditive category with HasCoproducts.{w} and homology, with independent object, morphism and simplex universes. The all-degree coefficient-generator law needs no homology assumption. This is separate from normalized tensor homology and asserts no singular or relative comparison.
