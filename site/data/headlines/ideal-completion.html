• Principal embedding. Order.Ideal.principalHom sends a to its principal ideal as a bounded lattice homomorphism; principalHom_injective shows it is injective. Its operation laws are available through the bundled homomorphism.
  • Frame and arbitrary suprema. The Order.Frame instance makes the ideal completion a complete Heyting algebra. The mem_sSup_iff theorem characterizes x ∈ sSup S by a finite set of elements, each in some ideal of the arbitrary family S, whose join is at least x. This includes the empty-family and bottom cases; it is not restricted to directed families.
  • Compact generation. Every principal ideal is compact, and isCompactElement_iff_eq_principal identifies all compact ideals as principal. The IsCompactlyGenerated instance expresses every ideal as a supremum of compact ideals. Compactness here is order-theoretic, not finiteness of the ideal's underlying set.
  • Prime ideals and Boolean-valued maps. For [Lattice A] and [BoundedOrder A], Order.Ideal.primeEquivBoolHom identifies prime order ideals with bounded lattice homomorphisms A → Bool. The characteristic map sends precisely the ideal to false; conversely BoundedLatticeHom.falseIdeal is its prime false fiber. Every such map is surjective. This includes the one-element lattice, for which both sides of the equivalence are empty.

These constructions and proofs build on mathlib's existing ideal, lattice and compactness infrastructure. They do not establish prime-ideal existence, spectra, Stone duality, a universal extension property, or generic partial-order completion.
